Personal Video Scheduler

A personal, single-user tool that uploads and schedules videos on its owner's own YouTube channel.

Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

This tool uses YouTube API Services. Use of it is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

What it does

Personal Video Scheduler runs on its owner's computer. It produces short videos from a list of topics and uploads them to the owner's YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API, setting each video to publish at a scheduled time.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. It is not a public service, has no sign-ups, and does not access any other person's account or data.

Google permissions

It requests a single permission, youtube.upload, used only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own channel.
