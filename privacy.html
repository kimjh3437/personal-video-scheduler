Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Personal Video Scheduler is a personal tool used by a single person, its owner, to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. This policy explains what it accesses and what it does with it.

YouTube API Services

Personal Video Scheduler uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

  • A Google OAuth token with the youtube.upload scope, granted by the owner.
  • The videos, titles, descriptions and tags that Personal Video Scheduler itself creates and uploads.

It does not read, collect or store any other YouTube or Google account data, viewer data, comments, analytics, contacts or personal information.

How it is used and stored

  • The token is used only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's channel.
  • The token is stored only on the owner's computer, in a file readable only by the owner's user account. A local record of uploaded video IDs is also kept on that computer.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. There are no cookies, analytics or tracking on this website.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party access. Deleting the local token file removes the stored credential. Uploaded videos can be deleted in YouTube Studio.
